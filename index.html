About
Jonathan Jenkins

Fifteen years in recruiting and consulting taught me one thing: the people who win consistently aren't smarter. They operate on a different layer of the game.

I spent a decade watching smart people lose to less talented operators who understood the architecture of decisions. I started writing to codify the patterns I kept seeing repeated across industries, negotiations, and competitive situations.

My first book, The 5 Laws of Agency, earned IndieReader Approved status and a 4.6-star rating. My second, The Quiet Advantage, applies structural thinking to competitive reality at a deeper level.

I live in New Hampshire.
